# State of the art

Step 3 of the review. For each question, the best evidence, kept apart from the practice most adopted. Upper bounds (what was achieved) are kept apart from limits (what was shown not to work). Sources are in [review](review.md).

**Shape:** [state of the art](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/a7f405f26f14cd188291e2a86b630121c4302a94/shapes/state-of-the-art.md)

## Q1 Runway and break-even

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | Carpenter 2002 | growth of most small firms is constrained by internal finance, in a panel of more than 1,600 firms | measured |
| Most adopted | Andreessen 2015 | gross and net burn from the balance of cash; runway left to the reader | convention, untested |
| Most adopted | Graham 2015 | the question default alive or default dead, asked early | convention, untested |
| Most adopted | OpenStax 2026 | break-even units as fixed costs over contribution margin per unit | convention |
| Limit | none found | no test of a runway forecast against outcomes | not found |
