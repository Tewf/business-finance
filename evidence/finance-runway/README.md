# Runway and break-even of a small venture

A first-pass multivocal review, read on 2026-11-04. It asks how long the cash of a small venture lasts when first revenue comes long after registration, what the funding sources cost, and what is measured of grants and accelerators. It ends on a named baseline.

- It holds 32 sources: 25 formal (one is the method paper) and 7 grey.
- Grey: 1 tier 2, 3 tier 1 and 3 tier 3; one scores below the threshold and is an example of practice only.
- Formal: most figures come from abstracts or summaries and are marked (secondary); six works were located only.
