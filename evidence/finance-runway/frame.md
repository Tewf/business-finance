# Frame: the problem and its names

Step 1 of the review, read on 2026-11-04. It states the problem, gives the field's names beside the practitioners' names, and says whether the repository's name matches.

**Shape:** [frame](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/a7f405f26f14cd188291e2a86b630121c4302a94/shapes/frame.md)

## The problem in one sentence

How long the cash of a small venture lasts when its first revenue comes long after its registration, and which costs can be made to wait for the event that justifies them.

## What the field calls it

Finance texts have words for the parts and none for the whole. Four levels meet.

| Level | The field's name | First met in |
|---|---|---|
| volume that covers costs | **break-even point**, from **cost-volume-profit analysis** (CVP) | OpenStax 2026 |
| cash held against spending | **internal finance** and **financing constraint**: growth limited by cash on hand | Carpenter 2002 |
| the source of cash by age of the firm | **financial growth cycle**: the right capital structure changes with size and age | Berger 1998 |
| spending released in steps | **staging** of finance: money given in rounds, each tied to news, with an option to stop | Gompers 1995 |
| forecasting under doubt | **planning fallacy**: plans are too optimistic; **prediction interval**: a forecast with its range | Buehler 1994; Hyndman 2021 |
| costs limited to what one can lose | **affordable loss**, from **effectuation**: control the downside instead of predicting the upside | Sarasvathy 2001 |
| funding by the owner's means | **financial bootstrapping**: meeting resource needs without external finance | Winborg 2001 |

## What practice calls it

- **Runway**: months until cash reaches zero. It is a practitioners' word. No formal source in these searches defines it.
- **Burn rate**: the rate at which cash falls. **Gross burn** is the monthly outflow and **net burn** is gross burn less incoming cash (Andreessen 2015).
- **Default alive** and **default dead**: whether current spending and growth reach profit before the cash ends (Graham 2015).
- **Nothing is paid before its event**: the maintainer's rule that a cost is chosen to start only at the event that needs it. The field's nearest names are staging and affordable loss.
- **Three-state plan**: before launch, at launch and at scale, each with the event that opens the next. The field's nearest name is the financial growth cycle.

## Does the repository's name match?

- Partly. "Finance and runway" uses the practitioners' word, which the formal literature does not. The field's own names are **entrepreneurial finance** and **small business finance**.
- The name is not changed here. The maintainer decides.
